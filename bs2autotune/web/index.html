Connect

Before you start
  • Connect the Bass Station II by USB and its line output to an input of your audio interface (line level, no instrument or mic gain stage, no effects).
  • Load an initialised patch on the synth, so that settings which cannot be reached over MIDI are neutral. Keep the mod wheel down.
  • Turn the volume of the synth up to about three quarters. The signal test below tells you if the level is right.
  • macOS asks for microphone access the first time: allow it for the app that runs this program (Terminal).
  • The app changes the patch that is being edited on the synth. Stored patches are not touched; you decide whether to save.

Calibration

Plays about a hundred short test notes (roughly a minute and a half) and measures how this unit responds: latency, tuning, filter and envelope curves, levels. Needed once; repeat it when you change the audio interface or its gain.
